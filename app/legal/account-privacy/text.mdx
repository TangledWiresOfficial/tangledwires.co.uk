## TangledWires Account Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the TangledWires Account Services.

We are TangledWires Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. Our company number is 16997164 and our registered address is 20 Wenlock Road, London, England, N1 7GU. Under UK data protection law, we are the "Data Controller" for your account information.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or legal guardian to review this policy with you.

### 2. Information We Collect

To provide your central login and keep your account secure, we collect the following information:

- **Account Information:** Your name, email address, username, date of birth (to verify you are at least 13), and your password (which is securely encrypted so we cannot read it).

- **Security and Technical Logs:** Your IP address, device type, browser details, and login activity (such as when you sign in, fail a login attempt, or reset your password). We also use strictly necessary cookies and local storage tokens to keep you signed in securely across sessions.

- **Service Connection Data:** Records of which TangledWires services you log into using your account.

### 3. How and Why We Use Your Information

Under UK GDPR, we must have a valid legal reason to use your personal data. We use your information for the following reasons:

- **To provide your account (Contract):** We use your account details to create your login and send essential service emails (such as password resets or updates to our Terms of Service).

- **To keep our systems safe (Legitimate Interests):** We monitor login logs and IP addresses to detect fraud, block bots, and protect your account from unauthorized access.

- **To meet legal duties (Legal Obligation):** We may process or retain certain records if required by English law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your account data for targeted advertising.

### 4. Sharing Your Information

- **Other TangledWires Services:** Because your account acts as a central login, when you use it to sign into another TangledWires service, we share basic identity details (such as your unique user ID, name, email address, and date of birth) with that service so it can check if you are allowed to log in to that service, and log you in if you are. Each service has its own Privacy Policy covering how it uses data inside that specific app.

- **Infrastructure Providers:** We use trusted third-party providers (such as cloud hosting and email delivery services) to run our authentication systems. They only process your data on our instructions and under strict security agreements.

- **Legal Authorities:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

### 5. Data Storage and International Transfers

Your account data is stored on secure servers located within the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area (EEA).

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your personal information for as long as your TangledWires Account remains active.

As explained in our Terms of Service, you can close your account and request the deletion of your data at any time by emailing [support@tangledwires.co.uk](mailto:support@tangledwires.co.uk). Once requested, we will permanently delete or anonymize your personal account data within 30 days, except where we are legally required to keep certain security or audit logs for a longer period.

### 7. Your Data Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

- **Correct** any inaccurate or incomplete information on your account.

- **Delete** your account and personal information.

- **Restrict or object** to how we use your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [support@tangledwires.co.uk](mailto:support@tangledwires.co.uk). We will respond within one month.

### 8. Complaints

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so we can resolve the issue. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK data protection regulator:

- **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**

- **Website:** [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)

- **Telephone:** 0303 123 1113

### 9. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified by email or when you next log in to your account when this happens.

### 10. Contacting Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, you can email us at [support@tangledwires.co.uk](mailto:support@tangledwires.co.uk).
