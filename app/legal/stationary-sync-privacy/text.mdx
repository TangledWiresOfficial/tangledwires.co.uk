## Stationary Sync Privacy Policy

### 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use Stationary Sync. It does not apply to your use of Stationary without using the Stationary Sync service.

We are TangledWires Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. Our company number is 16997164 and our registered address is 20 Wenlock Road, London, England, N1 7GU. Under UK data protection law, we are the "Data Controller" for your Stationary Sync information.

If you are under 18, please ask a parent or legal guardian to review this policy with you.

### 2. Information We Collect

To synchronise your train journey logs across your devices and keep the service secure, we collect the following information:

- **Account Identifier**: When you sign in via your TangledWires Account, we receive your unique account identifier (uid) to link your synced journeys to your account.

- **Train Journey Data**: The journey records you choose to synchronise, including journey timestamps, optional descriptions or notes, route segments (stations visited and railway lines used), and sync metadata (unique journey identifiers and deletion timestamps) used to keep your devices in sync.

- **Security and Technical Logs**: Your IP address and sync activity logs (such as connection timestamps and error reports) to keep synchronisation reliable and secure.

### 3. How and Why We Use Your Information

Under UK GDPR, we must have a valid legal reason to use your personal data. We use your information for the following reasons:

- **To provide the sync service (Contract)**: We store and synchronise your train journey logs, route segments, and deletion states across your connected devices.

- **To keep our systems safe (Legitimate Interests)**: We monitor sync logs and error reports to fix bugs, block abuse, and protect our servers from unauthorised access.

- **To meet legal duties (Legal Obligation)**: We may process or retain certain records if required by English law.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your journey data for targeted advertising.

### 4. Sharing Your Information

- **Infrastructure Providers:** We use trusted third-party providers (such as cloud hosting services) to run Stationary Sync. They only process your data on our instructions and under strict security agreements.

- **Legal Authorities:** We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

### 5. Data Storage and International Transfers

Your synced data is stored on secure servers located within the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area (EEA).

### 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your synced journey records for as long as you keep them in Stationary Sync or until your TangledWires Account is closed.

When you delete a journey in the app, its route details, timestamps, and notes are immediately erased from our active database. Only an empty deletion marker (containing the journey's random unique identifier) is kept so your other connected devices know to remove the journey during their next sync. Any backup copies of erased data are permanently purged within 30 days.

You can also request the deletion of your Stationary Sync data at any time by emailing [support@tangledwires.co.uk](mailto:support@tangledwires.co.uk), and we will permanently delete it within 30 days.

### 7. Your Data Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

- **Access** a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

- **Correct** any inaccurate or incomplete information.

- **Delete** your synced journey data and personal information.

- **Restrict** or object to how we use your data in certain circumstances.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at [support@tangledwires.co.uk](mailto:support@tangledwires.co.uk). We will respond within one month.

If you have concerns about how we handle your personal information, please contact us first so we can resolve the issue. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK data protection regulator:

We may update this policy from time to time. You will be notified by email or when you next log in to Stationary Sync when this happens.
